## H1/H2: extension beyond linear hypergraphs

Let n>=96 and 1<=d<=binom(n-1,2). A Boolean polynomial has degree at most three; each vertex belongs to at most d cubic monomials. Pair codegree is unrestricted. Coherent repeatable sign oracle, exact access, cubic support output. For any fixed epsilon>0 and d<=n^(2-epsilon), the following is a candidate uniform tight result (constants may depend on epsilon): quantum query Theta(d log n) with deterministic polynomial classical work; bounded-error classical binary value queries Theta(nd log n). More generally the lower bounds are Omega(d log(n^2/d)) and Omega(nd log(n^2/d)); upper bounds O(min(d log n,n^2)) and O(min(nd log n,n^3)). This leaves a potential gap near dense degrees. The earlier linear theorem remains a simpler constructive route for codegree one.

Lower bound: m=floor(nd/48), m>=nd/96. At step t<m at most floor(3t/d) vertices are saturated. At least 15n/16 vertices remain. Only previously used triples (at most t) need be excluded, not their pairs. Hence >=binom(n-floor(3t/d),3)-t >= n^3/20 legal triples for n>=96, since available-2>=7n/8 and t<=n^3/96. Each final graph has exactly m! orders. M >= (n^3/(20m))^m, log2 M >= (nd/96) log2(n^2/d). Apply the final-state span bound from LINEAR_QUERY_COMPLEXITY.md: q queries produce span dimension <=(2^n+1)^q, so constant worst-case success needs q>=log2(2M/3)/log2(2^n+1). Classical binary decision trees need log2(2M/3). The prior classical third-derivative upper bound and dense interpolation still apply. Balanced unit-pair queries give O(n^2) quantum fallback. Do not claim tightness in the unresolved dense transition region.

Upper bound by round-6 weighted moments: K=GF(2^m0), m0=max(2,ceil(log2(n+1))); choose beta in K with absolute trace 1 and extension F=K[u]/(u^2+u+beta). Assign distinct nonzero x_j in K and L_j=u+x_j. Unordered pair labels z_jk=L_j L_k=(beta+x_j x_k)+(1+x_j+x_k)u are nonzero and unique: they determine the sum s=x_j+x_k and product p=x_j x_k, hence the unordered roots of X^2+sX+p. Weight w_jk=x_j+x_k is nonzero. For each i, take 2d consecutive moments M_i(t)=sum_{jk in link(i)} w_jk z_jk^t, t=0,...,2d-1. With A_j=x_j L_j^t, B_j=L_j^t, the moment equals sum_{jk in link(i)} (A_j B_k+A_k B_j). For any binary bilinear multiplication plan uv=sum_l c_l a_l(u)b_l(v), query masks formed by applying a_l to all A_j and b_l to all B_j. Each contraction gives all n outputs simultaneously. Thus at most 8dR phase calls for R plan terms, with possible exact cache savings. No unknown tensor information is needed to form masks. This is nonadaptive acquisition under the degree promise.

For a link with r distinct labels and nonzero weights, the leading r-by-r Hankel matrix is V diag(w) V^T, where V is Vandermonde on the labels, and is invertible. The minimal linear recurrence has order r: the monic polynomial prod(X+z) annihilates the moment sequence, and a smaller recurrence would force a smaller rank of this Hankel matrix. With 2d moments Berlekamp–Massey recovers the minimal recurrence; scan the O(n^2) admissible pair labels for roots of the monic polynomial. Re-encode all moments and check three-way consistency. This costs polynomial classical work even with direct root scanning. Enumerating irreducible polynomials in degree O(log n) costs polynomial in n. Field representations can be converted by finding a root of the defining polynomial in F (at most O(n^2) candidates) and verifying the basis map. The scan deliberately trades CPU for simplicity; it is not the faster trace-splitting version in the historical archive.

**External theorem required for the optimal upper bound:** N. Bshouty, *Multilinear Complexity is Equivalent to Optimal Tester Size*, ECCC TR13-011 (2013), Corollary 3, p.9, https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2013/011/ (primary PDF https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2013/011/download/). It states a polynomial-time construction of a linear-rank bilinear multiplication algorithm for any fixed finite base field, including GF(2). Consequently R=O(m0)=O(log n) is available via that theorem. This is a composition of known results and the weighted link reduction, subject to independent review. Our executable `qcdt/general.py` instead uses explicit padded Karatsuba and verifies any supplied bilinear plan on basis pairs: R=O((log n)^log2(3)); actual default query bound O(d(log n)^log2(3)). The external linear-rank construction has NOT been implemented or timed; do not label actual code O(d log n). A supplied plan must be converted to the tower coordinates. Historical v0.5 already contains weighted moments, so no claim this reduction originated today.

Prior-art targeted searches: sparse cubic Boolean polynomial quantum parity oracle; bounded-degree hypergraph learning; sparse polynomial learning; finite-field multiplication linear rank. Compare Montanaro 2012 (https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ipl.2012.03.002, general degree-d polynomial learning), Montanaro–Shao 2022 (https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.TQC.2022.1, graph parity and graph-state models), Arunachalam et al. 2023 (https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.TQC.2023.3, phase-state copies), Bshouty above, Ballet et al. 2021 (https://doi.org/10.1070/RM9928, multiplication rank survey). None of these search results by itself establishes priority for QCDT. Further citation tracing and external review required. No claim that the general extension or tight bound is new to literature.

## H3: conditional word errors, amplification, fresh holdout

At every requested mask pair, conditional on all prior history, suppose P(measured n-bit word differs from ideal word)<=eta<1/2. Errors within one word can be arbitrarily correlated; error distribution may depend on history and masks. For R odd repetitions of a requested pair, a strict majority of correct words ensures every bit majority is correct. The conditional Hoeffding bound gives failure <=exp(-2R(1/2-eta)^2) (conditional mgf bound iterated over R indicators; no independence assumption beyond the conditional probability bound). For at most K publicly bounded adaptive probes, union-bound over probe index, conditional on previous history, gives failure <=delta if R>=log(K/delta)/(2(1/2-eta)^2) rounded up to odd. Cost <=4KR phase calls. This does not imply constant error probability under real gate noise; eta must be justified uniformly for every mask. Coherent repeatable V with independent resets and uniform bound from QUANTIZED_PHASE_FEASIBILITY.md is one sufficient case. When eta=0 one shot suffices. Stop early only once every bit has at least (R+1)/2 agreeing observations, so no possible continuation can change the planned bit-majority result. This saves calls without optional-stopping inflation. Pad hypothetically to R for the proof. Reject when budget exceeded.

Fresh unamplified holdout: alpha=21/32 is the minimum ideal full-word disagreement probability for a fixed wrong cubic candidate (H1_H4.md). True candidate mismatch <=eta; wrong candidate mismatch >=alpha(1-eta), even with mask-dependent corruptions. Gap g=alpha(1-eta)-eta>0 when eta<21/53. Draw independent uniform masks not known at candidate construction; compare empirical mismatch rate with midpoint. Conditional Hoeffding with R>=2 log(1/delta)/g^2 bounds each of false acceptance and false rejection by delta (for fixed candidate, not uniform post-selection on holdout). The smaller gap near threshold is expensive. No arbitrary/adversarial drift guarantee; a corruption that always changes the ideal tensor into a different one defeats identifiability. Fixed seeds for experiments do not provide adversarial security. This wrapper is not quantum error correction.

## H4: same phase-oracle access, public caps, no hardware

Compare reference QCDT, balanced dense derivative and single-bit Möbius interpolation with the same noisy diagonal V, no access to ideal values for acquisition. The single-bit method uses interference with |0> to get f(x) XOR f(0) and only queries nonzero words of Hamming weight <=3; relative constant cancels. Both dense methods get the same five fresh derivative holdouts as QCDT. All have public worst-case cost caps; per-method odd repetition counts derive from cap 4096 / public planned phase calls; all observations cost four phase calls for derivative or one for a single-bit query. Early stop when fixed majority irreversible. Same cap does not mean equal actual shots. Use exact Walsh probability distribution of four forward V calls and relative phase formula; simulate independent sampled outcomes, no gate noise/readout/routing or physical clock. Three fixed fixtures n=4 single, n=5 overlap, n=6 Pasch; four phase-offset scales; eight independently seeded offset vectors per fixture/scale; 288 method observations. Instance repeated across methods and scales; not independent hardware trials. All methods use known bounds. Record H and ideal all-to-all CX control counts (exclude oracle synthesis, measurement and routing, so not hardware gate comparison). A method may reject early. Retain negative results. None of this validates actual opaque access; #21 remains open.

## Evidence (local run)

1024/1024 exhaustive n=5 graphs, 1136 basis products in eight tower sizes, five additional arbitrary-codegree fixtures n=8..64. Four regression methods passed, including twelve end-to-end conditional word-noise runs. 500 random finite streams: early vs fixed majority always identical (7315 vs 7500 samples in benchmark). Conditional-word experiment 4800/4800 correct probes, eta=.05/.15/.25, 40 trials each with 40 masks, history-dependent error pattern; 119/120 correct candidates accepted and 0/120 wrong accepted in fresh holdout. These counts are empirical, not proof of zero error.

Phase experiment: all three methods 24/24 at each of scales 0,.1,.25. At .5: QCDT 15/24, dense 16/24, single-bit 21/24, no incorrect accepted in any condition. All actual calls under cap. QCDT used fewer calls on these fixtures due to conservative public plan and early rejection; not a matched-consumption or hardware advantage. At .5 total calls 19208, 54692, 51200 respectively across 24 trials; early rejection affects totals. Exact distributions derive from a simplified coherent model, not calibration. Do not adopt robustness as established under hardware noise.
